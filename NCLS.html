NCLS (No Class / Late Start) Policy

1. NCLS Definition & Classification

NCLS (No Class / Late Start) refers to any instance where a teacher:

  • Fails to join a scheduled class on time (even by 1 minute)
  • Does not attend the session at all (No Call No Show)
  • Cancels the session at the last minute

The following situations are considered under NCLS:

Category Description
Late Launch The teacher starts the session later than the scheduled time.
Forgotten Classes The teacher forgets a scheduled class and is reminded by a parent or staff member.
Missed Launch Leading to Cancellation The class is canceled because the session was not started on time.
Failure to Inform in Advance The teacher is unable to conduct a session but does not inform at least 8–10 hours in advance.
Repeated Technical Issues Without Notice Repeated delays or missed sessions due to technical issues without prior communication.
Last-Minute Cancellation The teacher cancels the class within 8 hours of the scheduled class time.

2. Communication & Escalation Process

First Instance: A discussion will be held to reinforce expectations around punctuality and attendance.

Second Instance: A formal warning email will be shared to document the recurrence.

Third and Subsequent Instances: A penalty equivalent to one class amount will be applied per occurrence.

3. Catch-up Class Requirement

  • Missed sessions due to No Show should be completed within the same calendar month.
  • If not completed, the penalty may increase to two classes.
  • In case of late start, the teacher is expected to cover the missed time.

4. Important Notes

  • Teachers are expected to strictly adhere to scheduled timings.
  • Advance communication is required for any unavoidable situation.
  • Consistency helps ensure a smooth learning experience for students.
